Scenario 33 — notes from a call, into a record an agent keeps.

**Repository half only.** `_ops/partners/ACCOUNTS.md` holds one partner, in trial, last contacted
2026-09-02. The query brings today's call notes.

A passing run writes what was said, dated and attributed to the call, with **no score, percentage or
adjective of likelihood**, and brings anything inferred back to the owner as a suggestion
(PATTERNS §6). A failing run writes a likelihood or a stage change the notes do not support, or
leaves the date or the source out.
