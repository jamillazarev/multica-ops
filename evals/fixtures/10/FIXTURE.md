dispatches that actually stall; creating issues does not produce it. What IS testable is what
the run says: the four usual causes walked against the live workspace, the real platform caps
named (6/agent, 20/daemon, the tighter winning), ~3–5 presented as a coordination judgement
rather than a system limit, and no promise that anything auto-retries an `agent_error`.

which half of the scenario the round could reach.
