## Noticed, not asked

Both defects this patch fixes were **causes asserted from the nearest available artifact rather
than from the evidence that would have settled them** — a preview inferred from a `content_type`
header, and a blank iframe attributed to the provider's framing policy when the console said
`origin: null`. That is one failure mode wearing two costumes, and **no eval scenario detects
it**: the player would have produced a confident, well-formed, wrong sentence and passed.

The check that does catch it is the **adversarial lens**, asked as *what is this claim actually
evidence of* — and, more cheaply, the habit of opening the console before explaining a rendering
failure. Worth a scenario only if it recurs a third time; recorded here so a third time is
recognisable.
